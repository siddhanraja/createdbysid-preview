Process

  1. Brief

    Purpose, audience and where it will run.

  2. Concept

    Script, references and storyboard to agree the idea.

  3. Production

    Generating, shooting or sourcing the shots.

  4. Refinement

    Edit, sound and grade, shaped by your feedback.

  5. Delivery

    Final files in every format and ratio you need.

About

I'm an AI video producer making short-form video for consumer brands: cinematic concept spots, product explainers and vertical social cutdowns.

My focus is the decisions between idea and export: the story, the look of each shot, what to keep, what to cut, and how to make many clips feel like one piece.
